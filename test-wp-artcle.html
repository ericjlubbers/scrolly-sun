Virtual Fencing · San Luis Valley
Colorado ranchers are swapping barbed wire for GPS collars, letting invisible boundaries redirect cattle with an audio cue and a mild vibration. The technology promises to cut fencing costs and give drought-stressed rangeland time to recover.
